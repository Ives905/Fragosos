The Room

Old city charm,
full-time hustle.

Fragoso's has been the neighbourhood's spot for a straight cue and a cold one for years — a low-key, no-frills room where regulars and first-timers end up at the same table by the end of the night.

Multiple well-kept tables, a kitchen that doesn't cut corners, and a screen big enough to make the away game feel like home. Come for the pool, stay for the wings.

The Renovation

A Whole New Room.

Fragoso's has been a Barton Street fixture for years — but it's no secret the old room had gotten a little rough around the edges. That's changed.

We gutted the space top to bottom, knocked down walls to open up the floor plan, and brought in professional-grade Predator ARC tables — each with its own dedicated overhead lighting, the same setup you'd find in a real tournament room.

The Equipment

Predator ARC Tables.

We didn't cut corners on the one thing that matters most — what's under the cloth. Fragoso's runs Predator ARC tables, the official table of the Pro Billiard Series.

Official Table — Pro Billiard Series Commercial Slate Bed Built-In Ball Return

Tournament-Grade Slate

A true commercial slate bed built for a dead-flat roll, rack after rack — the same standard the pros play on.

Ball Return System

No chasing balls around the room — the ARC's return system keeps the game moving so more racks get played.

Best in the City

Between the cloth, the cushions and the build quality, these are simply the best tables you'll find in Hamilton.

Whether you're in for a casual game or grinding it out in league play, you're shooting on the same equipment used on the pro tour.
